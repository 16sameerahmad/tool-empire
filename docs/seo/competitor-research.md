## Cross-cutting findings

- **Fortresses (do not fight head-on):** calculator.net (67.51M visits Sep 2026, Semrush),
  health.harvard.edu (authority), wordcounter.net (8.9M visits). Tulyro wins on long-tail,
  privacy (browser-only), and UX — not on head-term authority.
- **Cracks (weak domains in top 10s):** ncalculators.com, calculatorzilo.com (brand-new),
  base64decode.org (thin), invoice-generator.com (single-feature), ttsfree.com (niche).
  These are the pages where a correct, fast, well-structured new page can compete.
- **AI Overviews:** 39.4% of US desktop searches carried an AI Overview by June 2026
  (Comscore via iPullRank). Direct-answer intros + FAQ/HowTo schema are the honest
  response — no citation or Overview placement is promised.
- **Do NOT build (integrity):** AI detector, plagiarism checker, temp mail, social-media
  downloaders, AI headshot/voice/music — need backends, corpora, or carry ToS/copyright
  risk. A browser-only fake would be dishonest. (See strategy doc §5.)
